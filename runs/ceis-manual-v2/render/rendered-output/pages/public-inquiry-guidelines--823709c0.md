## PUBLIC INQUIRY GUIDELINES



### Searching files at your own location

If you have a Public Access Terminal (PAT), refer the public to it. If they are uncomfortable using the PAT or your location does not have one, you may do a search for them. Search fees are only charged to people who are not a party to the action.



### Searching files at other locations

You must exercise discretion when providing information from another registry location. If they are not a party to the action, refer them to the originating registry for further information. If an individual requests information about the terms of an order, you have the ability to print these details directly from CEIS; parties to the action are entitled to this information free of charge. Requests made by anyone who is not a party to the action are subject to the \$8 search fee. However, any specific details concerning the file should be referred to the originating court registry.

Please review the Provincial Court's [Access to Court Records Policy](https://www.provincialcourt.bc.ca/downloads/public%20and%20media%20access%20policies/ACC-2%20-%20Access%20to%20Court%20Records.pdf) that was updated December 2019 to ensure you are familiar with the access restrictions on files and documents.

You may also review the [Access to Provincial Family and Supreme Court Family files](https://portal.jag.gov.bc.ca/portal/page/portal/CSB_Home/document_repository/private/branch_info/courtrecord/guidelines-id-requirements-family-files-disclosure-information.pdf) and for Additional information review the [Records Access Policies page](https://portal.jag.gov.bc.ca/portal/page/portal/CSB_Home/private/Court_Record_Access/intro) .

# CEIS TRAINING

In order to obtain access to CEIS, training needs to be completed. To begin training, please contact our [Access Group](mailto:ceisjustin.accountaccess@gov.bc.ca).



### CEIS Training Steps

Training will be dependant on your Access Level required. There are 2 parts to training.

Clear directions will be provided directly from the access group within an email outlining what needs to be done.

1.  International Airport -- learner will register themselves. This can be done at any time.

To locate this from the home page, click:

Court Admin -\> hover over civil and click CEIS -\> click training -\> click CEIS International Airport. (this is the reading portion of the training)  

2.  The hands on training environment is also in this section called CEIS Training Application -- the learner will receive an ID to log on and use the data base.

3.  The learning scenarios for the Hands on learning in the training data base, is in the folder named Boarding Pass. The CEIS International Airport *(reading portion)* will indicate when to use the hands on training environment to put to practice what they just learned.

# PROTECTION ORDERS

The family law protection order is a civil order made in family court that contains a condition that affords safety and security to a specified person or persons. It must include a provision that restricts the activities of a party to prevent harassment, harm, or communication with another person. Orders must be made under s. 183 of the FLA to be called a protection order under the FLA. Protection or restraining orders may also be made in family court under the *Child Family and Community Service Act*, the *Family Maintenance Enforcement Act* and the *Adult Guardianship Act*.

It is a criminal offense for the person named in the order to disobey ("breach") it. If they do so, the police can enforce the order under the [Criminal Code](http://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-46/) and the individual may face significant consequences.



### Rules of a Protection Order

Protection Orders MUST:

- Have full complete names of the parties retrained and protected

- Have a date of birth for all parties retrained and protected

- Have complete addresses (if an address is mentioned in the POR)

- Have complete sentences (using proper punctuation)

- Be a separate order from any other terms such as child support or parenting time

- Be sent SAME DAY to the Protection Order Registry via POR Details in CEIS.

- Personally served if the restrained party was NOT present when the order was made (via Process Servers unless otherwise directed by your Judge)

### Returned Protection Orders

What to do when a Protection Order is returned from the Protection Order Registry.

First step is to read the comments from the Protection Order Registry.

Here are some of the common return reasons and how to fix it:

1.  ***Clerical Error***

If we make a spelling error on the Protection Order, or typo, we need to correct this by:

- Re-producing the original Protection Order with the corrected typo mistake.

- Saving the image over the original image

- Write a comment back to the Protection Order Registry that the typo has been corrected.

- And SEND the POR back to the POR Registry with a comment on what you corrected.

DO NOT create a new Protection Order with a new document number. We want to send the original document with the corrections back to the Protection Order registry for 2 reasons:

1 -- so the reconciliation report and history of this document is accurate, and 2 -- we should only have 1 active Protection Order on the file.

**IF the Order terms need to be amended,** *then this is different*. An Order Terminating the Protection Order (OTP) needs to be ordered and a New Protection Order needs to be spoken to.

1.  **Missing parties in the POR Details**

What we enter in the POR Details tab pushes to the Protection Order Application. It is important all the parties are listed so the names are searchable in the POR Database to ensure safety of the names individuals.

- If the POR comes back to add parties to this tab, follow these steps: [Steps to produce and send a Protection Order via CEIS](#steps-to-produce-and-send-a-protection-order-via-ceis) to add them correctly.

- Once all the parties are added, including children and d.o.b. if known then send the Protection Order back to the POR Registry.

- Send the POR back to the POR Registry with a comment on what you corrected.

  1.  **Restrained Party/Protection Party role reversal**

If an error was made and parties were placed under the wrong role

i.e. Jane Smith should be the restrained party (the person who is ordered to not go near, or talk to the other party).

John Smith should be the Protected Party (the one deemed to be in danger of the other party)

BUT we captured John Smith as the restrained party and Jane Smith as the Protected Party.

When this gets sent back from the Protection Order Registry because they noticed that the Order and the POR details are not correct, we need to

- Review the Protection Order.

- Correct the POR details by removing the names and readding them under the correct roles. See [Steps to produce and send a Protection Order via CEIS](#steps-to-produce-and-send-a-protection-order-via-ceis) on how to data enter on the POR Details tab.

- Send the POR back to the POR Registry with a comment on what you corrected.



### Daily Requirements

Reconciliation needs to be completed **on the next working day after the hearing where a Protection Order was made** to ensure that all Protection Orders have been entered and sent to POR. *(If PORs are common at your location, daily reconciliation is required)*

Detailed instructions for Supervisors/Administrators/Managers are located in the [Provincial Family Manual](https://csb.jag.gov.bc.ca/Manuals/fcm_manual.pdf) (Chapter 9) and the [Supreme Court Family Manual](https://csb.jag.gov.bc.ca/Pages/Manuals.aspx) (Part 25).

Detailed steps on How to Reconcile in CEIS is at [page 16.](https://csb.jag.gov.bc.ca/Civil%20Documents/CEIS/guides/HowToProcessPOR-Orders.pdf)

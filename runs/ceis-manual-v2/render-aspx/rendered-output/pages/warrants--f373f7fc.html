WARRANTS

Warrants are ordered by an Adjudicator to arrest an individual for a variety of reasons such as failure to appear to a court appearance after receiving a Summons.

It is important we are on top of warrants, adding and cancelling them in a timely manner.

Data Entering Warrants

If a warrant is issued by the adjudicator in court, its details need to be entered in the Record Results module under the Warrants tab, against the correct appearance.

The actual warrant document can also be produced directly from this screen.

Doc Type (1): Type of Warrant Ordered. (Only those types of warrants that relate to the level and class of the file will be displayed in the list of values.)

Filed (2): This date will default to the current court date, but can be overwritten, if necessary.

Immediate or With Notice Radio Button (3): If there is no Notice of the Warrant (Small Claims Court) then the Warrant is for immediate arrest.

Reason for Arrest (4): The purpose the warrant is issued.

Status (5): The Status of the Warrant (cancelled, executed, expired, held, issued or vacated)

Issued (6): This is the date that the warrant was issued against the party. By default, the current date is entered, but you can override it, if required, by typing in a different date.

BF Date (7): When a Notice is issued a BF date is implemented (Small Claims Court)

Failed to Appear Date (8): Appearance date the party missed.

Warrant Issued For (9): Party Name or Enter Name: Select a party from the drop-down list, or type in a new name if that person is not a party to the file.

Party Role: Once you enter the party name and tab (or click in the Party Role field), the first role for the party will populate in the Party Role field. You can override it, if necessary. Do not select a role for a person who is not a party to the file.

Click on the Comment tab to the right of each party name to enter any directions or comments made by the adjudicator with respect to that warrant.

NOTE – These comments are for internal purposes only and will not appear on the court list or the warrant document.

Click SAVE once you have entered all the information.

Once the warrant has been entered, it will appear on the Parties screen of the Maintain File module as "Issued" against the party's name under the "Warrant" column. The comments you entered will also appear on that screen.

Once a Warrant is issued, RED writing will appear on this file in CEIS.

Removing Warrants

It is very important that the warrant be removed immediately after it has been executed or cancelled. If the clerk does not remove it in the courtroom, the file should be flagged so the registry staff can attend to the removal immediately upon receipt of the file.

The FULL procedure is outlined in our Court Manuals with respect to which documents to create and who to send them to, depending on the circumstance and level of court.

To remove the Warrant Flag from CEIS:

Locate the Appearance date that the Warrant was issued, generally the Appearance with the result of “WI”:

Click on Record Results then the Warrants Tab and select the Warrant you wish to update:

And change the Status accordingly, then click Save:

This will remove the Warrant Flag in Red on the file:

Warrant BF Date Inbox

When a Warrant with Notice is issued on a Small Claims file, the person to be arrested is entitled to a period of time from receipt of the warrant to voluntarily appear on the warrant.

Over the years locations have established a variety of BF (bring forward) methods to keep track of the 21 (7 days per the face of the Notice + 14 days for service by regular mail) calendar days before the warrant could be sent to the sheriffs for apprehension

Civil eDocs introduces an electronic method of a BF diary. CEIS will automatically assign and keep track of the passing of the BF time for us. As well as notify that a voluntary appearance has not been made and that the warrant is eligible to be processed

From the Locate File screen, click the Warrant BF Dates button to access this new screen

A Warrant with Notice receives a BF date when the status of the Warrant is set to Issued and the Notice of Warrant of Arrest has been printed.

*If a Warrant does not have notice, making the warrant immediate, no BF date is assigned and the process described in this section of the guide does not apply.

*If a warrant has been cancelled, executed, expired, held or vacated it will not appear on the BF screen. A warrant requires the status of Issued to appear on the BF screen.

How to process a Warrant from the BF screen

  1. Select the Warrant and click the Process Button

CEIS will:

Select the correct Warrant of Arrest (WOA) document and Open the Document Details tab of that WOA

  1. Click the Produce Form button and follow the steps to create and print the Warrant.

Continue to distribute the paper warrant to the sheriff department per normal course.

Once the Warrant is printed it is eligible to be removed from the BF screen

  1. Return to the BF screen (Warrant Bring Forward Dates) and click Refresh, the file will then be removed from the screen

Change a BF Date

Often we need to change a BF date. For example, when the Notice of Warrant of Arrest is returned from Canada Post marked Not at this address.

Changing a BF Date is done from the Document Details tab of the Warrant (WOA) document.

1. Click into the BF Date field and edit the date.

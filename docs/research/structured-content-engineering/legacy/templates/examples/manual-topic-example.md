# Resetting Your Workstation Password

## Purpose

This topic explains how to reset your workstation login password when you
have forgotten it or it has expired.

## Before You Begin

- You must know your employee ID.
- You must have access to your registered recovery email or phone number.

## Procedure

1. On the workstation login screen, select **Forgot Password**.
2. Enter your employee ID when prompted.
3. Choose a recovery method (email or SMS) and select **Send Code**.
4. Enter the verification code you receive.
5. Enter and confirm a new password that meets the organization's password
   policy.

> [!WARNING]
> Do not share the verification code with anyone, including IT staff. IT
> will never ask you for this code over the phone or by email.

Illustrative image: password reset screen showing the verification code entry field.

## Expected Result

You are returned to the login screen and can sign in with your new
password immediately.

## Exceptions and Special Cases

If your account is locked after too many failed attempts, the
self-service reset above is disabled for 30 minutes. Contact the IT
Service Desk directly to have the lock cleared sooner.

## Troubleshooting

- **Verification code never arrives**: Confirm your recovery email or
  phone number is current in the employee directory, then request a new
  code — codes expire after 10 minutes.
- **New password is rejected**: Passwords must be at least 12 characters
  and include a number and a symbol; review the password policy link
  below.

# Plan part 2 potential consumers: Use SharePoint and Copilot Agents to Improve Knowledge Access and Learning

## Executive Summary

Many organizations have valuable knowledge stored across:

- Policies
- Procedures
- Manuals
- Training materials
- Knowledge articles

Finding the right information often requires searching multiple documents and reading large amounts of content.

This opportunity explores using AI-powered agents to provide consistent, conversational access to approved knowledge sources while potentially avoiding broad premium licensing through a consumption-based model. 【1-35fc9d】

---

# Current State

Users often need to:

- Search through multiple documents
- Read lengthy manuals
- Navigate complex procedures
- Interpret policies on their own
- Locate training material manually

Challenges include:

- Time spent searching
- Inconsistent answers
- Difficulty finding the right document
- Information overload
- Varying levels of user experience

【1-35fc9d】

# Future State

Provide a conversational AI experience grounded in approved organizational knowledge.

Users can ask questions naturally and receive:

- Consistent answers
- Guided assistance
- Contextual explanations
- Training support
- Step-by-step guidance

The agent becomes a knowledge-access layer over existing content rather than replacing the content itself. 【1-35fc9d】

# Strategic Rationale

User expectations are changing.

Many employees increasingly prefer:

- Conversational interactions
- Question-and-answer experiences
- Immediate responses

Over reading large documents from beginning to end.

An AI assistant can provide information in a form that aligns with these evolving expectations. 【1-35fc9d】

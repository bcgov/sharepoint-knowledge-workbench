Leave requests must be filed before the leave starts.

Managers approve or decline a request within five working days.

| Leave type | Notice |
| --- | --- |
| Annual | 10 days |
| Sick | As soon as possible |

Unused annual leave carries over for one year.

Expense claims are submitted with receipts attached.

Claims over the travel limit need a director's signature.

Reimbursement is paid in the next pay cycle.
